# Welcome post for the 📢 Broadcast channel

The Broadcast composer shows plain text, so this is written WITHOUT markdown
symbols (no `**bold**`). Copy everything between the lines and paste it into
Admin Panel → Broadcast → the composer, then post.

---

🪁 WELCOME TO KITE

This is our private workspace for running projects, staying accountable, and
getting help when something blocks you. Access is by invitation only — if
you're reading this, an administrator created your account personally.

WHAT THIS PLATFORM IS FOR
Every project manager here runs their own project. Kite is where you:
• record and track your project milestones
• flag problems early so they can be resolved
• request assistance from admins or the wider community
• keep a clear record of funded outcomes

HOW TO GET AROUND (the tabs at the bottom)

📢 Broadcast — official announcements from the admin team. Read-only, so
check here first for anything important.

🫶 Community — send encouragement to everyone with an emoji. Reactions only,
and they stay anonymous.

🗂️ Rooms — your private project rooms. You'll only see rooms you've been
added to. Chat with your team, and fill in any forms pinned at the top.

💬 DMs — private one-to-one messages. Tap anyone's name or picture anywhere in
the app to message them instantly. You can attach documents.

📊 Dashboard — your home base. Your milestones with countdowns, your funding
status, alerts from your administrator, and your account settings.

FIRST THINGS TO DO
1. Open the 📊 Dashboard tab and change your password to something only you
   know. You can also change your display name there.
2. Turn on notifications on the same screen so you don't miss messages or
   deadline reminders.
3. Install Kite on your phone so it opens like a normal app:
   • Android / Chrome: tap the ⋮ menu, then "Add to Home screen"
   • iPhone / Safari: tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen"

YOUR PRIVACY HERE
Kite is deliberately private. Other members cannot look you up, cannot see who
else is in a room, and cannot see your milestones, your funding or your form
submissions. What you submit is visible only to you and the administrators.

A FEW HOUSE RULES
• Photos and videos are posted by the admin team only. You can share documents
  in your messages.
• The Community room is for emoji reactions, nothing else.
• Keep milestone updates honest — flagging a blocker early is exactly what
  this platform is for. Being stuck is not a problem; staying silent is.

NEED HELP?
Send a direct message to an administrator or a staff handle at any time, or
raise it in your project room. Someone is usually on call.

Welcome aboard 🚀
